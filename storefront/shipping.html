Shipping and pickup

The parcel is the expensive part.

Prices on this site include tax. A single bar is $15 because someone has to carry it to a door. At a stall it is $13, because you carry it.

What you pay to ship

  • Ontario, under $58: $8.
  • Rest of Canada, under $58: $14.
  • $58 and over, after any code: shipping included.
  • The month is $58. That is the free-shipping offer. A code that drops the bag under $58 puts the parcel back on the bill.
  • We do not ship a single bar on our dime.

When it leaves

Orders placed before 2 p.m. Toronto time, Tuesday to Thursday, ship the same week. Otherwise within two business days of payment.

Christmas 2026: last ground ship we will promise is 15 December. Last Ontario expedited is 18 December. After that, pickup only, and only if a window is on the find us page.

Pickup

Pickup uses the stall price and costs nothing to ship. It appears in the bag only when a date is marked confirmed. Unclaimed pickup is refunded or shipped within 48 hours.

Tax

Ontario is 13% HST, included in the price. Other provinces are charged the GST or HST for the destination, still inside the price you see at checkout. We ship in Canada. Not outside it, yet.
