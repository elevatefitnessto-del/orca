Privacy

What we keep, and what we don’t.

This storefront keeps your order in this browser until a payments account and a real inbox are connected. Do not put a card number anywhere on this site. There is no field for one.

If you buy

We need your name, email, and, if we ship, your address, so the bars arrive and so we can write if a lot is recalled. Phone is optional. We do not ask about your skin, your health, or your birthday.

If you ask for notes

The checkout box is optional, and it is not checked for you. If you tick it, you are asking Ravine Bath, Toronto, for reorder notes and stall dates. You can unsubscribe by writing hello@ravinebath.ca. Buying something is not the same as asking for mail.

We do not buy lists. We do not sell the one we have.
